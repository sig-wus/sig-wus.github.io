Community initiative

Toward a Special Interest Group (SIG) on Wearable Ultrasound

SIG-WUS is a community effort exploring a Special Interest Group in wearable ultrasound: researchers, clinicians, and industry working toward shared standards, events, education, and open exchange.

    The open survey started at CEEUS 2026 closed its first round: 87 verified supporters from 15 countries and 67 organizations, including 30 PIs and team leaders and 14 industry supporters.
